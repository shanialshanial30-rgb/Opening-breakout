How the trigger works

At the US open, the app records the opening price. It calculates open × 1.015 for BUY and open × 0.985 for SELL. The first level reached wins and the opposite side is ignored.

Demo only. This build does not connect to Trading 212 and cannot place real orders. The next stage is live market data, then Trading 212 Demo, then (only after testing) Live.
