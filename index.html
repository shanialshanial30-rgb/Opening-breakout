How it works

The app waits for the US regular session. Once the market is open, it records the official day-open value supplied by Twelve Data and calculates BUY at open × 1.015 and SELL at open × 0.985. The first trigger reached for a stock creates one simulated trade; the opposite side is then ignored for that stock.

After entry, the live WebSocket price stream checks TP +2.5% and SL −1.5% on every incoming price event. No one-minute polling is used for live prices.

DRY RUN ONLY. This app does not connect to Trading 212 and cannot place real orders. It is a simulator for testing the strategy and data feed.
